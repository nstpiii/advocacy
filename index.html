Purpose & Direction

Mission & Core Objectives

General Objective

To provide accessible educational support and mentorship to learners in the community, helping them overcome academic challenges and develop effective study habits for long-term success.

Core Advocacy Objectives

01

To help students improve their understanding of difficult subjects through free tutoring and review sessions.

02

To build the confidence and positive attitude of students toward learning.

03

To promote the value of education among students, parents, and the community.

Specific Objectives

Four Pillars of Student Support

1

Academic Support

  • Help students improve their understanding of difficult subjects through free tutoring and review sessions.
  • Assist learners in completing assignments, projects, and school requirements on time.
  • Enhance students' reading, writing, comprehension, and problem-solving skills.
2

Study Skills & Mindset

  • Teach effective study techniques, time management, and note-taking strategies.
  • Build students' confidence and positive attitude toward learning.
  • Encourage regular school attendance and active participation in class.
3

Community & Empowerment

  • Promote the value of education among students, parents, and the community.
  • Provide basic school supplies to learners who lack resources.
  • Foster a culture of volunteerism — where older students/community members help younger ones grow.
4

Sustainability

  • Establish a peer-to-peer learning system that continues beyond the program duration.
Background Information

Understanding the Community Need

Locality

University of Baguio Vicinity & Nearby Barangays

This project will be conducted in the vicinity of University of Baguio, Baguio City, serving nearby barangays and communities where many students reside. The area has a large student population but limited after-school learning support.

Problem

Limited Guidance After School Hours

Many students in the community struggle to understand difficult lessons and finish schoolwork because they have no one at home to guide them. There are few free tutoring services available, so learners fall behind and find it hard to keep up with their classes.

People

Elementary & High School Learners

The project benefits elementary and high school students from nearby communities, especially those from low-income families. Without extra help, these students are the most affected — their grades suffer, and they lose confidence in their ability to learn.

Stakes

Breaking the Cycle of Academic Difficulty

If this need is left unmet, students may continue to struggle, lose motivation, and some may eventually drop out of school. Education is the foundation of a better future, and without support, the cycle of difficulty continues from one generation to the next.

Response

Kaagapay sa Pag-Aaral

Kaagapay sa Pag-Aaral provides free tutoring, mentorship, and study assistance. It bridges the gap between classroom lessons and home learning, helping students improve their grades, build good study habits, and stay in school.
