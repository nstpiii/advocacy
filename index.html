Ang Kaagapay sa Pag-aaral

Ang Kaagapay sa Pag-aaral is a tutoring and mentorship program at the University of Baguio. It offers free tutoring, review sessions, and mentorship to students who struggle with difficult subjects, helping them improve their understanding, build confidence, and stay motivated in school.

Purpose & Direction

Mission & Core Objectives

General Objective

To provide accessible educational support and mentorship to learners in the community, helping them overcome academic challenges and develop effective study habits for long-term success.

Core Advocacy Objectives

01

To help students improve their understanding of difficult subjects through free tutoring and review sessions.

02

To build the confidence and positive attitude of students toward learning.

03

To promote the value of education among students, parents, and the community.

Specific Objectives

Four Pillars of Student Support

1

Academic Support

  • Help students improve their understanding of difficult subjects through free tutoring and review sessions.
  • Assist learners in completing assignments, projects, and school requirements on time.
  • Enhance students' reading, writing, comprehension, and problem-solving skills.
2

Study Skills & Mindset

  • Teach effective study techniques, time management, and note-taking strategies.
  • Build students' confidence and positive attitude toward learning.
  • Encourage regular school attendance and active participation in class.
3

Community & Empowerment

  • Promote the value of education among students, parents, and the community.
  • Provide basic school supplies to learners who lack resources.
  • Foster a culture of volunteerism, where older students and community members help younger ones grow.
4

Sustainability

  • Establish a peer-to-peer learning system that continues beyond the program duration.
Background Information

Understanding the Community Need

Locality

University of Baguio Community

This project focuses on the University of Baguio community, where many students from elementary, junior high school, and senior high school live or study. The area has a large student population but limited after-school learning support.

Problem

Academic Difficulties Without Support

Many students struggle to understand difficult lessons and complete their schoolwork. Without proper support, these challenges affect their learning progress and make it harder to keep up with their studies.

People

Elementary, Junior & Senior High Learners

The project serves students from elementary, junior high, and senior high school who need additional academic assistance, especially those who have limited access to guidance at home or free tutoring.

Stakes

Risk of Losing Motivation

If these struggles continue, students may gradually lose motivation and even consider dropping out of school. Education is the foundation of a better future, and without support, the cycle of difficulty continues.

Response

Ang Kaagapay sa Pag-aaral

The program provides free tutoring, mentorship, and study assistance, along with a supportive learning environment. It helps students become more confident, motivated, and capable of achieving their academic goals.

Anticipating Obstacles

Challenges & Solutions

1

Low Student Participation

Some students may be hesitant to join because of shyness, lack of confidence, or lack of awareness about the program and its services.

Solution

Promote the program through teachers, school announcements, and social media. Use the website to share project information, explain its benefits, and provide an accessible way to express interest.

2

Limited Budget and Resources

The program may lack funds for learning materials, printing, school supplies, and maintaining the resources needed for tutoring and the website.

Solution

Seek donations from university departments, organizations, and community members. Maximize free digital learning materials and use available school supplies and free website resources.

3

Lack of Volunteer Tutors

There may not be enough volunteers available or knowledgeable in different subjects to accommodate all participating students.

Solution

Recruit volunteers from the University of Baguio, particularly education-related students and qualified student organizations. Coordinate with teachers for guidance and use the website to organize requests.

4

Tutor and Student Schedule Conflicts

Volunteers and students may have different class schedules and responsibilities, making it difficult to find a common tutoring time.

Solution

Create flexible weekly schedules, coordinate availability in advance, and use the website to receive consultation requests and arrange suitable mentorship schedules.

5

Different Learning Needs and Difficulties

Students may have varying academic levels, learning speeds, and subject difficulties, making it challenging to give everyone equal attention.

Solution

Conduct needs assessments to identify specific learning difficulties. Group participants by grade level and subject needs, then provide appropriate materials and individualized guidance when necessary.

Impact

Expected Outcomes

Improved Academic Performance

Students are expected to understand difficult subjects better, improve their study habits, and become more capable of completing their schoolwork.

Increased Confidence and Motivation

Through regular tutoring and mentorship, students are expected to become more confident in their abilities and develop a more positive attitude toward learning.

Accessible Academic Support

Students will have access to free tutoring, review sessions, and mentorship opportunities that address their individual learning difficulties.

Increased Awareness Through the Website

The website will let students learn about the project, view available services, contact organizers, and request academic assistance or mentorship consultations.

Stronger Community Involvement

The project encourages volunteerism and cooperation among university students, teachers, parents, and the community in supporting students' educational needs.

Continuous Guidance and Support

Through tutoring sessions and the website, students can seek academic guidance, ask questions, and receive continued assistance.
